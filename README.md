# Meraj

I build automation systems that work in the real world, not just in test environments. Right now I lead IoT and robotics engineering at Rapid Solutions International in Dubai, where I design industrial systems from the ground up. That means firmware talking over MQTT, FastAPI backends handling data from hundreds of connected devices, and the dashboards the people on the floor actually use to run things.

I didn't start here. I started building websites for small local businesses in a small city. That gap between making a simple shop site and running backend and IoT teams across industrial sites taught me more than any course ever could. The fundamentals always travel further than the frameworks.

What I care about is simple: systems that are fast, reliable, and actually useful. The best moment I've had on the job was when one of our systems flagged an equipment failure twelve hours before it happened. No alarms, no drama. It just quietly saved the client from a costly shutdown. That's the whole point for me. Good engineering solves real problems without needing applause.

I like simple systems your team can debug at 2 AM over clever ones that only impress people in a design meeting. I like knowing both the firmware and the backend, because understanding the full stack is what makes you genuinely useful instead of just technically employed. I like writing things down, because clear documentation is what holds a team together under pressure. And I like building tools to fix the things that annoy me. That's how my VS Code extension for ABB robots started, and it grew into a full IDE that talks to IRC5 and OmniCore controllers.

What I don't like is over-engineering. I once turned something that should have been a monolith into a tangle of microservices, and I've designed for failure ever since. In industrial environments unreliable networks are normal, so I stopped expecting perfect conditions and started building around them. I'd rather ship something honest and maintainable than something impressive and fragile.

Mostly I work in Python, FastAPI, TypeScript and Vue, around MQTT, ESP32, Raspberry Pi and ABB robotics, but the tools matter less than the thinking. If you're working on something in IoT, backend systems, robotics, or industrial automation, I'd be glad to talk.
